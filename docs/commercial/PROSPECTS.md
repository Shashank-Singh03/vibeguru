# Agency lead research: 30 candidates, not 30 qualified buyers

Researched **8 October 2026 (Asia/Calcutta)** using agency-owned service pages, portfolios and one primary case-study PDF. No organizations or people were contacted, no customer application was tested, and no private contact data was collected.

`A` = the primary material explicitly associates React web delivery with a relevant named application or case. `B` = React/application service evidence exists, but exact sustained-session portfolio fit needs confirmation. `C` = content access or technology/app attribution remains unresolved. These tiers describe **public evidence**, not purchasing qualification or independent verification of agency claims.

Current evidence counts: **16 A, 9 B, 5 C**. This is a useful 30-lead research shortlist; it does not claim 30 confirmed small-agency prospects. Even A-tier leads require discovery of an active project, actual pain, a decision maker, budget, safe access and an unmet gap. Several larger specialists are explicitly retained as benchmarks or possible partners rather than first buyers.

Start with the first eight for fit research. They are ordered by apparent offer relevance and prospect-identification usefulness, not by verified team size, buying likelihood or quality of their work. Recheck the primary source before personalizing an unsent draft.

## Source and method limits

- Agency pages are first-party marketing accounts, not independent evidence that the app currently runs as described or that a client result occurred. No reported agency revenue/conversion/performance gains are relied on here.
- Direct pages were opened and relevant text inspected where the research tool could extract it. Evolvion timed out; Digisoft extraction was unavailable; Pagepro returned metadata without the case body; Polcode extraction was inconsistent. Those remain C-tier.
- React Native does not establish React browser fit. Next.js delivery does not establish that Vibe Guru's current client-side route exercise works on that router.
- A service page with an illustrative dashboard scenario is not proof of a completed client project. Miracuves is intentionally marked for verification.
- A bank's CMS site, mobile chart app, unrelated Angular dashboard, or company React website is weaker fit evidence than a sustained-use React application. Do not silently promote such leads.
- Existing audit/QA/performance offerings can make a firm a substitute or potential partner rather than a customer. No lead is assumed to lack quality practices.
- Source URLs and paraphrased observations are retained in [PROSPECTS.json](PROSPECTS.json) for updating research. No raw copyrighted page copy, outreach history or personal-data list is stored.

## Qualify before offering a pilot

1. Confirm a current React **web application** project and a handover date or actual runtime/repeated-use incident.
2. Identify the technical owner and person authorized to approve a $300 scoped pilot; do not invent names.
3. Ask what existing QA/Playwright/DevTools/monitoring already covers and which useful gap remains.
4. Confirm one stable app version, safe local/staging access, up to ten routes and two explicit journey outcomes.
5. Confirm a developer will review/fix issues and that the six-hour total/one-rerun scope is acceptable.
6. Record real payment/action/repeat evidence in [DISCOVERY.md](DISCOVERY.md). Public portfolio fit alone is not commercial traction.
